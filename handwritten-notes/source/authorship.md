# Stroke source and review record

`pen-strokes.svg` is original digital pen-stroke artwork authored for this delivery. Its full-word paths were drawn as coordinate strokes; no handwriting typeface, downloaded handwriting, or photographed person's writing was used. It is released under MIT with the original content. `input/page-01.png` is a raster rendering of these paths, not a photograph of physical notes. The editable SVG retains the strokes and the unresolved mark.

The page reads “Shelf check”, “Keep 12 boxes.” and “Move [unclear] shelf.”. The overlapping mark after “Move” has no recoverable intended word; it is intentionally left unresolved. It is not a crossed-out word. No struck-out words occur on this page. For replacement pages the policy is to omit struck-out words and record each omission in a reviewed decision record before running the command.

What I checked: I read the raster alongside the transcript. “Shelf check” and “Keep 12 boxes.” are readable, and the mark after “Move” does not resolve to a word, so the transcript keeps `[unclear]`. This is my own reading for legibility and correspondence, not a measured accuracy study and not a review by anyone else. No transcription speed or handwriting-recognition accuracy is claimed. Review time was not measured. A person must read replacement images and prepare/review `transcript.json` before running the command.
