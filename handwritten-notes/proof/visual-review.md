# What the screenshots show

This page describes only what is visible in the images. It says nothing about how the files were made or tested.

**The PDF render** ([`preview.png`](../output/preview.png)): the heading “Shelf check”, the line “Keep 12 boxes.” and a bullet “Move [unclear] shelf.”. The rest of the page is blank.

**The first-screen picture** ([`media/hero.png`](../media/hero.png)): the rendered PDF page cropped to its content, showing the heading “Shelf check”, the line “Keep 12 boxes.” and the bullet “Move [unclear] shelf.”, with a thin grey border. The caption under it says the page I typed it from is a demo page I drew for this case. The drawn page itself ([`input/page-01.png`](../input/page-01.png)) shows “Shelf check”, “Keep 12 boxes.” and “Move”, a scribbled mark, “shelf.” in pen strokes; it is shown further down the README.

- [Pages layout, 390 wide](https://github.com/Ardholt/doc-pipeline/blob/main/handwritten-notes/output/page-previews/pages-390.png): the heading, the result sentence, the line about the pen strokes, the typed page picture with its text legible at full width, and its caption about the demo page.
- [GitHub-style frame, 1366 wide](https://github.com/Ardholt/doc-pipeline/blob/main/handwritten-notes/output/page-previews/github-1366.png): the frame header and file list, the heading, the result sentence, the line about the pen strokes, and the typed page picture.
