Record D09-14. Round 09. Sheet 134.

A small repair begins with a decision about the boundary of the work. Identify the loose or damaged part and keep adjacent parts in their original order. Use a tray for removed fittings so that every piece can be accounted for at the end.

The tray for item K134 showed a displaced protective strip. The strip was returned to its marked position after the edge was checked. The note records both the observation and the action.

After the repair, check the function that was affected and the parts disturbed during access. Record what was changed rather than writing only that the item is fixed. A future inspection should be able to identify the repaired area without taking the item apart.
