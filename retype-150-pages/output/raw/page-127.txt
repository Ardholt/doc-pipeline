Dry storage depends on routine observation as well as closed lids. Check the seal before moving a container and look for a change in the paper liner. A small curl at an edge may be the first visible sign that conditions have changed.

The tray for item K127 showed a displaced protective strip. The strip was returned to its marked position after the edge was checked. The note records both the observation and the action.

Separate observations from explanations. Record a damp patch as a damp patch; do not record a leak unless its path has been found. Move the affected material to the holding shelf and preserve the arrangement that will help the next inspection.
