TOOL RETURN / 123

Record D09-03. Round 09. Sheet 123.

A tool returns to service only after its condition has been checked. Brush away loose material and place the tool on the inspection mat. Compare the working edge with the condition recorded before use, and record any new mark in ordinary words.

The tray for item K123 showed a displaced protective strip. The strip was returned to its marked position after the edge was checked. The note records both the observation and the action.

A clean surface does not prove that a tool is ready. Check the joint, handle and retaining pin as separate parts. If a part is missing, mark the whole tool as held; never leave it in the ready tray beside complete tools.
