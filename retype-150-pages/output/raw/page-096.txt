LABEL READING / 096

Record DO7-06. Round 07. Sheet 096.

Read a label from left to right before copying its code. Similar characters are a reason to slow down, especially where a faint stroke might be part of a letter. Compare the code with the item record only after making an initial reading.

The tray for item K096 showed a light scuff near the rim. The scuff was compared with the earlier condition note. It was kept in the record even though it did not prevent the next check.

Keep the old label when a replacement is fitted. Write down the reason for replacement and check that the new label identifies the same item. Clear lettering is helpful, but an attractive label attached to the wrong container creates a larger problem.
